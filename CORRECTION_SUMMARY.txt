================================================================================
GRID POSITIONING
================================================================================

OLD SYSTEM (Offset-Based):
  Formula:  (round((x-8)/16)*16)+8
  Returns:  8, 24, 40, 56, 72... (offset positions)
  Origin:   (8,8)
  Result:   Sprites appeared shifted left

NEW SYSTEM (Boundary-Based):
  Formula:  16*round(x/16)
  Returns:  0, 16, 32, 48, 64... (tile boundaries)
  Origin:   (16,16)
  Result:   Sprites perfectly centered
